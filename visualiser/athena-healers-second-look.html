02 · The Problem

The problem is not missing data. It is missing access to specialist reasoning.

Evidence already exists across open sources (CIViC, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov). The true bottleneck is lacking a multi-specialist team to reason across it.

Expertise Distribution Gap
Barrier: Fragmented Isolation — General oncologist manages rare/exhausted case alone without a specialist team.
Target User Profile
GENERAL ONCOLOGIST
District or Tier-2/3 hospital practitioner with no in-house MTB down the hall.
RARE / EXHAUSTED CASE
Standard first-line protocols exhausted; second-line literature and regulatory access urgently needed.
ZERO CLOUD SUBSCRIPTION BUDGET
Cannot absorb per-case US-academic SaaS pricing or expensive data licensing.

05 · Core Differentiator

ATHENA tracks what changed.

A finding depends on both patient state AND evidence state. Both can break assumptions and invalidate existing findings.

Trigger State Change
RE-EVALUATE ONLY WHAT CHANGED
Indexed lookup avoids re-running every case.
Dependency Chain Simulating: Patient Change
Mechanism: When EGFR alteration arrives, NoAlterationIn(EGFR) fails. Only dependent findings re-evaluate.

Engineered for commodity public hospital infrastructure with strict, transparent scientific limits.
