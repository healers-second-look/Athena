## Participant Information Sheet and Consent Form

### Study title and purpose

**Title:** Evaluation of the Athena clinical decision-support system versus usual information tools for synthetic oncology case review.

**Purpose:** This study compares how medical oncologists review synthetic HR+/HER2− breast cancer case scenarios when using their usual clinical information resources versus the Athena system. The goal is to measure usability, confidence, and decision quality on entirely fictional cases. No real patient care decisions are made in this study.

### What you will be asked to do

You will complete **two study sessions**, each lasting approximately **60 minutes**, at times convenient to you. In each session you will review **synthetic** breast cancer case scenarios.

The study uses a **within-participant design** with two conditions:

1. **Usual care:** You may use your normal clinical tools (e.g. UpToDate, NCCN, other references you typically rely on).
2. **Athena full:** You will use the Athena web application provided for this study.

All cases are **synthetic**. They do not describe real patients. Outputs from the study systems are **not clinical advice** and must not be used to guide real patient care.

### Data we collect

- Profile information you provide at enrollment (name, contact details, specialty, practice context).
- Your responses, confidence ratings, and management decisions on each case.
- Timings and interaction logs within the study application.
- Chat messages if you use conversational features in the Athena condition.
- **Optional:** screen recording during sessions, only if you explicitly consent below.

Your study activities are linked to a **pseudonymous study ID** (e.g. P01). Identifying details are stored in a **separate database table** from study events and are accessible only to authorised members of the study team.

### Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is **voluntary**. You may **withdraw at any time** without giving a reason. Withdrawal will not affect your professional standing or employment.

You may request deletion of your identifying details, subject to legal and ethical requirements for research record-keeping.

### Risks and benefits

**Risks** are minimal: mainly the time required to complete sessions. There is no physical risk. Case content may discuss oncology treatment concepts familiar to your practice.

**Benefits** to you may include reflection on decision-making workflows. You will not receive direct clinical benefit. Aggregate results may inform development of decision-support tools.
